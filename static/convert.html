    How it works

    1. Choose a PDF. It is sent to this server over an encrypted connection.
    2. It is converted, usually in about a second, into a Word document.
    3. Your download starts, and the moment it has been sent, both files are deleted from the server.

    Questions

    What does it keep from the PDF?

    Headings, paragraphs, tables, images and formatting, as editable Word content rather than pictures of the page. It is good but not a perfect copy: dense multi-column layouts, unusual fonts and very large tables are the usual trouble spots, so check the result against the original.

    Is my file uploaded, and is it kept?

    It is uploaded, because the conversion runs on this server. It is written to a temporary folder, converted, sent back, and the folder is deleted as soon as your download has been sent. The file's name is not recorded. If you would rather a document never left your device, the two editors on this site work entirely in your browser.

    Can it convert a scanned PDF?

    No. A scan is a picture of a page, so there is no text in it to convert. The converter recognises a scan and says so, rather than giving you a Word file full of images.

    How big a file can I convert?

    Up to {{MAX_MB}} MB. You can also convert just some of the pages by entering them in the Pages box, for example 1-5 or 2,4,6.
