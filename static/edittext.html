What this will and will not do

It replaces text on a line and keeps the font, size, colour, alignment and any underline. If the new wording does not fit it will move the rest of the line, shrink very slightly, wrap, or move the line to free space — and if none of those are safe it refuses and tells you why, rather than producing a mess.

It will not reflow a whole paragraph, and it cannot edit scanned pages, justified lines, or Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic or Hebrew text. Every edited file is checked before you can save it.

Ready to save

How it works

  1. Open a PDF. It is read by this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Click a line and change the words. Press Preview to see exactly what will be saved, then Keep this change.
  3. Carry on, on any page. Every change you keep is held together, and all of them are saved in one file.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Editing and previewing are free: make as many changes as you like and see the finished document before deciding anything. Is my document uploaded?

No. The PDF is read by this browser tab and edited there, using a PDF engine that runs on your own device. When you pay for a clean copy, the server is told a fingerprint of the file so it can remember the payment — never the file, and never its contents.

Will it keep the font?

It keeps the font, size, colour and alignment of the line, and any underline. If your new wording is longer, it moves the rest of the line along, sets it very slightly narrower, or wraps it. The preview shows exactly what will be saved, and the panel beside it says which font was used.

Can I change more than one line, or lines on different pages?

Yes. Keep each change as you go and move between pages freely. Everything you have kept stays on screen, and all of it is saved together. You can also go back to a line you have already changed and change it again.

What can it not do?

It does not reflow a whole paragraph: it changes one line at a time. It cannot edit scanned pages, the lines of a justified paragraph (the last line of one is fine), or Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic or Hebrew text. When an edit would not be safe it refuses and tells you why, rather than saving something broken.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in an ordinary mobile browser. The page is fitted to the screen, so pinch to zoom in before tapping a line if the text is small.
